# Verified Puzzle Detector Engine — Single-Source Inventory

Date: 2026-08-30

## Verdict

The requested opening, trap and endgame detection capability exists, but it is
not coach-complete and is not governed by one proof contract. The implementation
must extend the existing canonical sources below. It must not create a new chess
taxonomy, opening book, trap library, endgame library or tactical geometry library.

The repaired authored coverage now includes 41 publishable opening lessons plus
38 recognition aliases, 36 unique forced-trap lessons, 19 opening plans/gambits, and 20
verified endgame lessons. The personal curriculum derives 41 opening skills,
two individual trap skills, 14 trap-family skills, 14 endgame skills, two
basic-mate skills, three strategic-concept nodes and one coached-development
node. The application registry now contains 48 deterministic adapters: seven
opening, three trap, 30 endgame and eight positional candidates. Registration
does not grant product authority; 47 are Shadow and the superseded broad rule-
of-the-square adapter is Disabled while independent review is pending.

The earlier production learning evidence still confirms the historical wiring
gap: 7,363 opening skill records had no applied-game evidence; the four then-
existing trap/trap-set records had none; 73 of 81 endgame records had applied
evidence. Those figures predate the repaired registry and describe stored
evidence coverage, not detector accuracy. Shadow detectors cannot write new
player mastery. The current reproducible map is generated by
`backend/scripts/report_coach_detector_capabilities.py`.

## Verified capability boundary

### Openings

Opening identity and exact authored-decision matching cover all 41 publishable
lessons and require at least two player decisions. The detector can also retain
a stored-best off-book move as a sound-deviation candidate without calling the
authored line mastered. Stored-best candidates exist for castling, central pawn
control, development while attacking and basic development. They deliberately cannot
explain the positional reason for every deviation, prove a transposition, or
generalize an opening-to-middlegame plan. All 19 opening plans now have an exact-
position, stored-best application detector; it remains Shadow and makes no claim
for analogous positions.

### Traps

Trap sequence and exact verified-defense matching cover all 36 unique forced traps.
The adapter grades only the authored victim continuation, the stored-best setter
continuation, or the exact authored defense when stored best agrees. Fourteen
rating-banded trap families (31 traps) are curriculum-selectable; five additional
traps remain available in Explore until their families receive data-locked
selection envelopes. The adapter cannot yet generalize a tactical mechanism,
move-order transposition, or superficially similar harmless sequence. Those
claims must come from geometry and stored engine evidence, not the trap name.

### Endgames

Every one of the 20 authored endgames now derives an exact-position, stored-best
transfer detector from the canonical lesson. Broader geometry candidates cover
opposition, Lucena, Philidor, king centralization, creating a passer, active
rooks, stopping promotion and the two basic mates. Sixteen lessons have existing
rating envelopes and curriculum nodes. Good/bad bishop, opposite-colour bishops,
knight blockade and knight-versus-bishop remain Explore-only until selection
ranges are measured. Exact-position detection is lesson evidence, not proof that
the player can generalize the technique.

### Tactics, geometry and positional play

Reusable proof families now verify free pieces, trapped pieces, forks, pins and
skewers with stored payoff, discovered attacks, removing the defender, forced
mate and exact back-rank mate before a specific puzzle label can be admitted.
Stored-best Shadow adapters also cover knight outposts, open files, seventh-rank
rooks, central pawn breaks, minority attacks, isolated-queen-pawn play, king
luft and a narrow king-tuck prophylaxis case. Broader strategy remains Shadow
until the positional evidence protocol and two independent coach reviews pass.
The remaining problem is consolidation of older recognizers with different
evidence and consumers, not authoring another duplicate taxonomy.

## Add-cost rule

Adding one opening, trap, endgame lesson or chess concept must require one
authored-source edit. A detector adds executable proof for a canonical concept ID
and an independent verifier; it does not restate the lesson catalogue. Derived
indexes, caches and surface payloads must be regenerable from their canonical
source. Guard tests will fail when a retired recognizer or duplicated content
table becomes a new source of truth.

## Pre-code consequence

The shared admission contract may reference canonical fact IDs and versions, but
must not embed copied opening lines, trap sequences, endgame lesson text or
geometry tables. Consolidation order will be selected from production exposure
and false-label risk after the data lock.
