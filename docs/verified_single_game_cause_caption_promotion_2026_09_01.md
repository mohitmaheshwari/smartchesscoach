## Manual review method

For every fire, the reviewer checked the starting FEN, played move, best move,
both available stored lines, capture ledger, cause object, caption, principle,
visual relationships, and verifier output. For every negative, the reviewer
checked that this narrow family lacked a complete exact mate or material cause;
positional possibilities without a proved cause remained abstentions.

The review caught defects that the first automated pass missed:

- equal recaptures were incorrectly described as hanging pieces;
- favorable and material-neutral sequences were incorrectly offered as the
  cause of an engine loss;
- a verified mate was subordinated to a pre-existing loose piece;
- a captured piece that appeared later in a stored line was checked only
  against the initial board;
- a best-move branch claimed to defend a piece that existed only in the played
  branch.

Each defect was repaired in the canonical extractor or verifier and locked by
an exact regression. The packet was then regenerated and reviewed again.

## Promotion boundary

This evidence authorizes only concrete single-game sentences already proved by
the cause contract: a legal material loss, a losing exchange, a material
opportunity in a fully replayed stored line, or a terminal mate in a fully
replayed stored line.

It does **not** authorize the system to name a fork, pin, trap, opening idea,
endgame technique, positional weakness, recurring habit, or knowledge gap.
Those require their own detector and promotion packet. It also cannot drive a
coaching plan, update mastery, prescribe training, or persist a learner claim.

The packet is from one authorized player and is sufficient for chess-claim
precision over varied positions, not for population preference or ranking.
Multi-player blinded validation remains required before changing the visible
ranking formula or leaving the validation cohort.

## Reproduction

Run `backend/scripts/build_verified_single_game_cause_promotion_packet.py` in
an environment with read-only access to the configured database. The script
reuses the production review reconstruction and reports zero engine runs, LLM
calls, and database writes. Human verdicts are stored in the versioned gold
packet and are not generated by the script.
