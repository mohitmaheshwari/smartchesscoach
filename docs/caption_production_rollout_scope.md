# Caption Production Rollout — Scope

**Status:** draft for Mohit signoff · 2026-07-08
**Goal in one line:** make game-review captions *reliably teach the moves that matter, and never lie* — in production, on every analyzed game — so the app is worth opening to real users.

---

## The problem, plainly

Every strong caption Mohit has seen this week (poisoned-pawn traps, hang warnings,
the 24-caption game) was generated by **hand-running a scratch script** and writing to
the DB. Real users hitting `/game/:id` still get the old principle-based captions, which
are uneven: some teach, many are thin ("X was better"), a few were wrong (the `a4 =
centre` misfire). The engine-verified approach is proven in prototype but **is not in the
product**.

Three gaps, and only three, stand between "impressive demo on Mohit's game" and
"launch-worthy":

1. **The good captions aren't in the production render path.** They must run
   automatically at analysis time, on every game, for every user.
2. **There's no "what did MY move do" purpose detector.** We explain the user's
   *mistakes* and the *opponent's* moves, but not a purposeful own move (attacks a
   piece, takes a square, defends a weakness, gains space). This is the "a4 could be an
   attack" gap.
3. **"Ready to launch" is a vibe, not a number.** We can't currently say "captions are
   good enough" with evidence.

---

## Where the code actually is (grounding)

- `generate_game_decryption_v5()` iterates the game's moves and, per move, calls
  **`build_move_teaching_decision()`** (`services/caption_pipeline.py`). Its
  `_decision.text.caption` becomes the stored `decryption_v5_data[i].caption`.
- That function is the **single source** for both review (V5) and PWC. New teaching
  logic goes here — not a parallel system.
- It works from **stored analysis** (`best_move_uci`, `pv_after_played`,
  `pv_after_best`, evals) + `python-chess` on the board — **no live Stockfish at render
  time**. Live engine is available only at *analysis time* and at *distill time* (offline).
- A predicate can already emit `evidence.why_text`, which **overrides** the static
  principle cue (caption_pipeline ~L4168). This is the hook the verified templates use.

**Consequence:** the verified generator's slots must be derivable from stored PVs +
board geometry + SEE. Where a slot needs data we don't have stored, it **abstains** —
never invents.

---

## The bar (non-negotiable)

Same discipline as the distill loop, restated for launch:

- **Zero false claims shipped.** Every factual clause (hangs, attacks, defends,
  captures, "wins the X") is re-derived from the board by an independent verifier at
  render time. Fails → abstain that clause, or the caption.
- **Eval-aware severity.** A move that stays winning is never "blunder." winning→still-
  winning = "missed a stronger move"; winning→equal = "mistake"; winning→losing / hung
  decisive material = "blunder."
- **Very-simple teaching voice.** Short sentences, easy words, no chess idioms, minimal
  notation, one reusable fundamental on real mistakes.
- **Coverage tiers, never silent on a real mistake:** (1) rich engine-verified teaching;
  (2) verified purpose/contrast; (3) honest thin fallback ("Y was better, you're now
  worse") — but *only* where a deeper why isn't engine-decidable. Routine moves stay
  quiet/light.

## Workstreams

### WS1 — Productionize the verified generator (the unlock)
Move the proven scratch logic into `caption_pipeline` as verified predicates that emit
`why_text`, gated behind a flag (`VERIFIED_CAPTIONS`). Cover, in priority order:
- **own hang** (piece_safety) — proven, 14/14 verified.
- **opponent inaccuracy** (plan + why-it-fails + your reply) — proven, recapture-safe.
- **own-move purpose** (WS2).
- **eval-aware "missed a stronger move" / "mistake"** thin-but-true fallback.
Each ships with its verifier checker **before** the claim is allowed to render.

### WS2 — Own-move purpose detector (the a4 gap)
For a purposeful own move, name what it does, engine/board-verified:
- **attacks**: move gives a new attack on an enemy piece worth pressuring
  (`enemy_sq in board_after.attacks(to_sq)`, piece ≥ minor or forces a real concession).
- **defends**: move now guards an own piece/pawn that was loose before.
- **develops**: minor off the back rank toward the centre.
- **takes a key square / restricts**: strict, verified square control (only if clean).
- If none verify → abstain the purpose (don't pad).
Positive captions on good moves; secondary clause on mistakes.

### WS3 — Data-measured acceptance
- Sample **N real user moves** (Mohit + Parth + Shobhit + a held-out set), render through
  the new path.
- Report: **% of significant moves with a verified-true teaching caption**, **false-claim
  count (must be 0)**, coverage by tier, and a shortfall list.
- **Pick the launch threshold AFTER seeing the distribution** (threshold-before-
  distribution is a sin). Gate the flag-flip on it.

---

## Rollout order (prove → wire → measure → flip)

1. **Prove** WS2 own-move purpose on real games (scratch, verified-truth %) — as we did
   for piece_safety + opponent moves.
2. **Wire** the proven predicates into `build_move_teaching_decision` behind
   `VERIFIED_CAPTIONS` (default OFF). Verifier-first per claim type.
3. **Backfill** a batch of real games; **measure** (WS3).
4. **Flip** the flag on the server once the bar clears; bump `V5_COACHING_VERSION` to
   force re-render.

---

## Risks / honest caveats

- **Stored-PV depth caps tactical specificity** (known). Deep combos may only say "a
  forcing line that wins" — acceptable; abstain the deeper claim.
- **Positional whys abstain by design** (king safety, pawn structure). We do not force a
  why we can't verify. That's a *feature*, not a shortfall.
- **"Launch-worthy" is broader than captions** (onboarding, the import→analyze→train
  loop, reliability). Captions are the differentiator and the current weak point, so they
  are the right first battle — but this scope does not claim to cover the whole launch.

---

## Out of scope (this pass)
- Live-engine-at-render (too slow; not needed).
- Re-analyzing games to deepen PVs (separate effort; abstain in the meantime).
- Non-caption launch work (onboarding, billing, etc.).
