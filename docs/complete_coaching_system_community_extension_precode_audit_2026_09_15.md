## Six hard gates

1. **Literal UI mockup — PASS.** The approved master scope shows the assignment, bounded chapters, shared review and private follow-up with literal player copy.
2. **Pattern headline — PASS.** The community study now leads with the memorable chess question, not the source game, opponent or SAN move.
3. **Data-derived choices — PASS.** The 80-game packet locks the two-chapter floor, exact rating-band requirement and balanced formula. It rejects spectacle, popularity and guessed weights.
4. **Behavior-changing success — PASS.** The final behavior is completing a guided study, applying its lesson and returning to the personal next action. Completion remains assisted evidence and cannot claim transfer.
5. **Deferred work stays deferred — PASS.** Generic feeds, public profiles, reputation, leaderboards, open matchmaking and unmoderated human coaching remain absent. Human matches are a bounded later workstream in this same program, not part of the Shadow code slice.
6. **Mohit sign-off — PASS.** Mohit explicitly locked the extension into the current phase and then said “approved, go ahead.”

## Forecasted bottlenecks

1. The 80-game packet has broad raw teaching supply, but neutral chapters may shrink sharply after current authorization and source-player personalization removal.
2. A player-visible pool cannot use cross-user Chess.com imports under the locked source policy.
3. The current activity baseline cannot support honest instant matchmaking.

The Shadow packet must measure the first bottleneck before visible integration. Silence is preferable to widening admission or copying personalized source text.

## Allowed first edit
