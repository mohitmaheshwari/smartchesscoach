The user's vision is to create a hyper-personalized, data-driven chess coaching application that functions as a human-like AI coach. The central goal is to move beyond generic analysis and provide Socratic-style, contextual feedback tailored to the user's playstyle.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (User-defined):**
-   **AI Teaching Coach:** An AI that plays against the user, selecting instructive moves to create learning opportunities.
-   **Active Teaching Framework:** The coach must provide real-time, conversational guidance before, during, and after each move, explaining plans, patterns, and principles.
-   **Chess Understanding Service (CUS):** A core service that computationally analyzes a position's strategic and tactical elements (pawn structure, piece activity, tactical motifs) to provide factual, structured data to the LLM for explanation. This avoids LLM hallucination.
-   **100% Accuracy through Human-in-the-Loop:** A system for human coaches to review, edit, and approve AI-generated explanations, building a verified and defensible knowledge base.
-   **Phase-Aware Coaching:** The coaching style must adapt based on the game phase (opening, middlegame, endgame), calculated using a continuous Stockfish-style piece value system, not a fixed move count.
-   **Deep Endgame Knowledge:** The coach must identify specific endgame types (e.g., Rook vs. Pawn, Opposite Color Bishops) and teach the relevant principles and techniques, a key differentiator from other apps.
-   **Human Coach UI/UX:** The application must feel like a personal coaching session, prioritizing game reflection, providing behavioral insights, and using a warm, collaborative, and conversational tone in plain, simple Indian-English.
