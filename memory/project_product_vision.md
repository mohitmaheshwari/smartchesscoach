---
name: Product Vision — Coaching Philosophy
description: Core design principles for Play with Coach — teach thinking, not moves. Fundamentals-first, guided discovery, make players feel better.
type: project
---

**Core philosophy:** Teach them to THINK, not what to THINK. Never spoon-feed moves.

**Key principles:**
1. Intervene on every mistake/blunder — not just 2-3 critical moments, not every move
2. Give PLANS, never moves — "Your knight needs a better square" not "Play Nd5"
3. Follow up interventions with a plan/direction
4. Opening theory = ideas, not lines. "Why this opening exists" and "the general plan"
5. Make them feel better — the player should leave every game feeling they understand chess better
6. Guided discovery (Socratic) — ask questions, give hints, let them find the move
7. Track fundamentals per game — are they checking threats, calculating, castling early, developing pieces?

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't show engine eval numbers
- Don't give them the move
- Don't overwhelm with interventions on good moves
- Don't teach 10 openings — focus on their best 1-2

**How to apply:** Every feature in Play with Coach must pass this test: "Does this make the player think, or think for them?"
