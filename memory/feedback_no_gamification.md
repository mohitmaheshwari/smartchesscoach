HARD product rule locked by Mohit 2026-05-19: **ChessGuru is a serious chess-improvement platform. No gamification. Ever.**

**Why:** The target audience (1200-1500 plateau players who pay ₹199/month) is buying serious coaching. Gamification mechanics (streak-or-die counters, badge collection, leaderboard rank chasing) turn a coaching relationship into a slot machine. Different product, different psychology, different retention story. Mohit's framing: "i want it that ways" — locked.

**How to apply:** No matter how well-intentioned, do NOT add:
- Win-streak banners or counters (current HomePage has one — should be removed)
- Daily-streak counters or "don't break the chain" prompts
- Badges, achievements, trophies
- Leaderboards (per-user, per-friend, or global)
- Points, XP, levels, ranks (other than actual chess Elo from real games)
- "You're in the top X%" comparisons
- Time-pressure mechanics ("solve in under 30 seconds!")
- Streak-protection mechanics

What IS allowed (because it's just honest reporting, not gamification):
- Progress trend ("2 fewer blunders than last week") — factual, not competitive
- Win-rate per opening — factual
- Mistake-pattern trends — factual
- "You've mastered X principle" if measured against real solve-rate data — factual, ladder of competence (not arbitrary points)
- Mood-aware tone ("you've been on a losing streak; here's encouragement") IF it's genuine coach voice, not a manipulative mechanic. **But evaluate carefully — this is the line where coaching becomes mechanic.**
